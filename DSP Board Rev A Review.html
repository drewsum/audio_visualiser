High fix before ordering

  • H1J1903 Digi-Key PN is the wrong connector
    35-PRPC003DAAN-RC-ND is a Sullins PRPC003DAAN-RC, a 2×3 male header (confirmed on DigiKey). The footprint is PinSocket_1x06_P2.54mm_Vertical for the encoder module socket.
    Use a 1×6 female socket PN (e.g. Sullins PPPC061LFBN-RC family) and recheck height vs. the case.
  • H2Fab outputs and zone fills are stale
    gerbers/ and gerbers.zip are from 21 Oct 20:34; the .kicad_pcb was saved 24 Oct 13:03. The saved board also has unrefilled zones: 5 clearance errors on the ~{RESET_LED} via at (93.68, 87.07) against GND / +3.3V on In1–In4/In6 (0.071 mm vs 0.15 mm). All 5 disappear when DRC runs with zone refill.
    Refill all zones (B), save, rerun DRC, then regenerate gerbers, drill, BOM and CPL from the current board.
  • H3R406 (20 mΩ 3.3V shunt) has an invalid Digi-Key PN
    P16010CT-ND does not resolve to a product on DigiKey (search falls back to unrelated parts). The P160xx prefix is Panasonic's ERJ-P03 0603 series, not a 0612 wide-terminal 20 mΩ part. R406 is in series with the whole +3.3V rail, so a wrong part here breaks the board.
    Pick a real 0.02 Ω 1% 0612 part (e.g. ERJ-B2CFR02V or ERJ-B1CFR02U) to match R207's family, and confirm footprint.

Medium fix or consciously accept

  • M1TCA9801 vs TCA9803 is ambiguous
    U803/U1807/U1809 are valued TCA9801 (1.1 mA B-side current source), but the symbol is project_lib:TCA9803, the datasheet link is tca9803.pdf, and the schematic notes say "3.3 mA current source". The B-side source is the only pull-up on I2C_SDA/SCL and the panel buses, so the choice sets rise time and how hard every slave must sink (RILC ≤ 150 Ω).
    Pick one part. With several devices, the Adafruit cable and a 30-pin ribbon on the panel side, TCA9802/9803 give more rise-time margin. Make value, symbol, link, notes and PN agree.
  • M2Encoder module pull-ups sit on a no-pull-up bus
    J1903 (Adafruit 5880) lands on I2C_SDA/SCL, which is U803's B side. TI says no external pull-ups on SDAB/SCLB because extra current defeats the IILC low-detection. STEMMA QT boards normally carry 10 kΩ pull-ups.
    Verify the module's pull-ups and cut/remove them, or move the module onto its own A-side bus. Also confirm J1903 order (pin 1 Vin, 2 NC, 3 GND, 4 SCL, 5 SDA, 6 INT) against the module pads.
  • M3dsPIC I2C1 side relies on internal pull-ups
    The TCA9801 A side needs a pull-up. R802/R804 are DNP and the note says to use internal pull-ups. The dsPIC33A CN pull-up is 175–545 µA (≈6–19 kΩ). With D601 (16 pF/line), the header and the buffer, that works at 100 kHz but not reliably at 400 kHz.
    Run I2C1 at 100 kHz, or populate R802/R804 (~10 kΩ) and confirm the PICkit still programs. Keep the footprints either way.
  • M4Panel PGOOD inputs float
    LEFT/RIGHT_LED_POS3P3_PGOOD_BUF and LEFT/RIGHT_VLED_PGOOD_BUF go straight from J2101/J2102 to 1G97 inputs with no resistor on this board. Unplugged panel means a floating CMOS input; if the panel's PG is open-drain it never reads high.
    Add a 10 k pull-down on each (or pull-up to +3.3V if the panel PG is open-drain).
  • M5TPD3S014 has no input capacitor
    The datasheet asks for ≥0.1 µF on IN close to the IC. Net-(U904-EN) (IN + EN) only has FB901 to VBUS; cable inductance plus the bead can ring on hot-plug.
    Add 0.1–1 µF from U904 IN to GND.
  • M6Input TVS is DNP and the chosen part clamps too high
    D201 SMAJ24CA is DNP. If fitted, its clamp is ~38.9 V at Ipp, above the TPS25947's 28 V absolute max on IN.
    Fit a TVS with standoff >13.2 V and clamp <28 V (SMAJ15A/SMAJ15CA class, Vc ≈ 24 V).
  • M7Anti-alias filter does less than its note claims
    Each Sallen-Key stage is fc 23.2 kHz, Q ≈ 0.73 (C1603/C1601 = 1000/470 pF). Two equal-Q stages are not a 4-pole Butterworth: about −3.8 dB at 20 kHz and only about −5 dB at 22.05 kHz, so at 44.1 kS/s it barely suppresses aliases, contrary to "must filter hard".
    Either oversample (the ADC is fast) and decimate digitally, or rework to Q 0.54 / 1.31 with a lower fc. Update the note.
  • M8Speaker input clips at ~12 Vrms
    21:1 divider, in-amp gain 2, output limited to 1.6 ± 1.6 V, so full scale is ±16.8 V peak differential (≈18 W into 8 Ω). Louder amps clip in U1401/U1402 before the digipot can attenuate. Each R1401–R1404 (0402, 1/16 W) is near its rating above ~35 Vrms per leg.
    Confirm the amplifier range you expect; consider ~40:1 and 0603/0805 resistors for the high-voltage leg.
  • M9AVSS isolated from ground by a ferrite
    FB702 puts a bead between GND and GNDA_DSP (AVSS). The audio chain and the 1.6 V reference used as ADC negative input are referenced to GND, so AVSS noise shows up as ADC error. Microchip ties AVSS to VSS.
    Make FB702 a 0 Ω (keep the footprint as an option).
  • M10Ferrite in the PICkit ground return
    FB602 sits between J601 pin 3 (GND) and board GND. Ground bounce on PGC/PGD edges can break programming and debug.
    Replace FB602 with 0 Ω.
  • M11C203 bulk cap voltage derating
    C203 is 100 µF 25 V in a Kemet 7343-43 case (399-8410-1-ND). If it is a T491 MnO2 tantalum, 13.2 V exceeds the usual 50% derating.
    Confirm the series; prefer a polymer (T520/T521) or a 35 V rating.

Low cleanup, documentation, minor margin

  • L1ERC: PWR_FLAGs on op-amp-driven nets
    All 4 ERC warnings are #FLG01701/#FLG01702 on +1.6V_AREF_LEFT/RIGHT, which U1701 pins 1 and 7 already drive.
    Delete both PWR_FLAGs.
  • L2~{RESET_LED} polarity naming
    R1205 pulls it high in reset; U1203 (NOT) lights D1203 and U802 (NOT) holds I2C_EN low. Firmware drives it low to turn the LED off and enable I2C. The logic works but the net is effectively active-high.
    Rename to RESET_LED or add a note.
  • L3Note: TCA980x EN pull-up is to VCCA
    Three notes say "Internal 250k pullup to VCCB". Datasheet 9.3.4 says VCCA. Same 3.3 V here.
    Correct the text.
  • L4Note: PGOOD divider at 13.2 V
    R208/R209 give 13.2 × 3.65/13.65 = 3.53 V, not 3.58 V. Other eFuse note values check out.
    Change to 3.53 V.
  • L5Note: PGA gain at reset
    Midscale wiper gives −5k/(1k+5k) = −0.83 (−1.6 dB), not 1, because R1502/R1503 add to the input leg.
    Fix the note, or set the startup wiper code in firmware.
  • L6MCLR series resistor above recommendation
    R609 = 1 k; DS70005629 §2.5 recommends R1 ≤ 470 Ω.
    Use 470 Ω.
  • L73.3V regulator rating vs. intent
    TPS62150A is a 1 A part, but the telemetry note sizes the rail for 3.1 A and the symbol is TPS62130 (3 A). Output caps are 2×10 µF 0805 (≈12–14 µF at 3.3 V) vs. 22 µF recommended.
    Confirm the load budget and intended part; consider a third 10 µF or a 22 µF.
  • L8VOS sensed after the shunt
    U402 VOS ties to +3.3V downstream of R406; the datasheet says sense at the output cap. Effect is small at these currents.
    Accept, or move VOS to the C406 node.
  • L9POS12_PGOOD drives the dsPIC before 3.3V exists
    The R208/R209 divider (≈2.7 kΩ Thevenin, 3.2 V) feeds RC7 while VDD is still 0 V, injecting ~1 mA through the ESD diode until the buck starts.
    Add ~10 k series into U701 pin 26, or accept.
  • L10ENCODER_INT has no pull-up
    J1903 pin 6 goes straight to RC6.
    Enable the internal pull-up in firmware or add one if the module's INT is open-drain.
  • L11CP2104 VPP capacitor
    C906 is 10 µF; Silicon Labs specifies 4.7 µF, only needed for OTP programming.
    4.7 µF or DNP.
  • L12TXB0102 VCCA can exceed VCCB
    VCCA is the CP2104 regulator (3.0–3.6 V), VCCB is +3.3V. VCCA ≤ VCCB can be violated by tens of mV. Your schematic note already flags this.
    Accept, or power VCCA from a rail guaranteed ≤ +3.3V.
  • L13Unused op-amp bias wastes 3.3 mA
    U1701C/D are biased by 1 k/1 k dividers (R1701–R1704).
    Use 100 k.
  • L14Pull-downs on actively driven nets
    R401, R605, R801, R807, R1801–R1805, R1907 sit on nets a push-pull output always drives.
    Optional: DNP to save parts.
  • L15Symbol / datasheet / footprint mismatches
    X601 (ASE) links ASV.pdf; L701 is a Murata DFE252012F on a Würth PMCI-252012 footprint; D601/D2101–D2104 use the PESD3V3L5UY symbol for PESD5V0L5UYF; U402 uses the TPS62130 symbol.
    Align fields; check the L701 land pattern against Murata's drawing.
  • L16Test points without a PN
    TP201, TP202, TP203, TP401, TP601, TP901 have no Digi-Key PN; TP701/TP1701/TP1702 use 36-5005-ND.
    Fill in or mark as bare pads.
  • L170402 10 µF on USB rails
    CL05A106MP6NUN8 (10 V X5R 0402) keeps only a fraction of 10 µF at 3.3–5 V.
    Use 0603/0805 where it matters (C902, C903, C910).
  • L18USB shield capacitor rating
    C911 is 10 nF 50 V 0402 between CHGND and GND.
    Consider a ≥100 V (ideally 1–2 kV) part.
  • L19DRC: J901 hole clearance
    8 errors: shield PTH to NPTH peg at 0.266 mm vs JLC rule 0.28/0.30 mm. Comes from the JAE land pattern.
    Confirm with JLC DFM or nudge the shield slots within JAE's tolerance.
  • L20DRC: dangling vias
    I2C_SDA (41.71, 36.63), POS12_PGOOD (42.01, 50.70), POS3P3_PGOOD (59.82, 51.49).
    Delete the stubs.
  • L21DRC: silkscreen
    199 overlaps, 26 silk-over-edge (J201, J601, J901), D201 silk on J201 pads, non-mirrored copper text "8" on B.Cu at (128.17, 115.23).
    Clean up; mirror the B.Cu text.
  • L22Floating copper zones
    Zone "EmptySpace" with no net on F.Cu, B.Cu and In5.Cu reports isolated fill.
    Tie to GND or remove.
  • L23dsPIC core buck loop size
    L701 and C717 centers are about 6 mm from U701's center; Microchip asks for a compact Lx/VDDCORE loop on the same side.
    Check the Lx–L701–C717–VSS loop on the layout.
  • L24JTAG pins carry live signals
    RB1/TMS carries +1.6V_AREF_RIGHT (pull-up active on an erased part); TCK/TDO/TDI carry the LED SPI lines.
    Set JTAGEN = 0 in config bits.
  • L25Panel back-powering during bring-up
    LED_BUF_OE enables RUN, SPI, LAT and BLANK together, so the SPI lines can drive an unpowered panel.
    Firmware: hold SPI/LAT/BLANK low until the panel reports PGOOD.
  • L26Digi-Key coverage was partial
    The DigiKey API credentials on this machine are not configured, so only J1903 and R406 were checked live. The rest were checked by distributor prefix and MPN pattern against value, footprint and rating.
    Upload the BOM to DigiKey's BOM manager before ordering.

Checked and found correct

  • dsPIC33AK256MPS105 48-VQFN pinout vs DS70005629C Table 3; EP to GND; buck parts (10 µH, 10 µF VDDCORE, 10 µF SWVDD); decoupling count matches the note.
  • PPS notes match the nets (SCK1/SDO1/SCK2/SDO2, OCM1/OCM2, U1TX/U1RX, REFO1, INT1); ICSP on PGC1/PGD1.
  • Differential ADC: AD1AN1/AD1ANN2 and AD2AN1/AD2ANN2 can pair (independent PINSEL/NINSEL).
  • All 21 74LVC1G97 instances decode to the intended function (NOT, BUF, AND, gated PGOOD, NAND for LEDs).
  • TPS259474L: UVLO 10.37/9.42 V, OVLO 13.90/12.63 V, ILIM 6.07 A, blanking 0.84 ms, ramp ≈60 ms, PG 10.43 V. All match the note.
  • TPS62150A pinout; FB divider gives 3.328 V; 2.2 µH at 2.5 MHz; output discharge circuit (Q401/Q402) works.
  • INA231 CAL/LSB math (0x0800, 0x0A92) and shunt polarity; MCP9804 pinouts.
  • I2C addresses unique: MCP4451 0x2C, encoder 0x36, PCA9536 0x41, INA231 0x40/0x43, MCP9804 0x18–0x1A.
  • RCA divider 4.022:1 and speaker 21:1 notes correct; MCP6N11 pinout, gain 2, EN/CAL wiring.
  • MCP4451 QFN-20, TS3A24159 (Low = RCA), REF35160 pinout and 1 µF output cap, TXB0102 DCU, TPD3S014, CP2104 pin map, USB-C CC 5.1 k.
  • 74VHC541 pinout, unused inputs tied, OE pulled to disabled in reset.
  • Every logic input is driven or biased except the items above; no net has two push-pull drivers.
  • Logic levels: dsPIC VIH is 0.8·VDD (2.64 V); every dsPIC input sees a 3.3 V CMOS source or the 3.2 V PGOOD divider (≥2.79 V at the PG threshold).
  • Schematic/PCB parity clean; no unrouted nets; no courtyard overlaps.
